IDENTITY / 01

First contact shows the server’s SHA-256 fingerprint before credentials are sent. You approve and pin it; changed keys are rejected.

02 / SECURITY SEQUENCE

Trust is a decision.
Not a default.

CyberSSH pauses on an unknown server, presents its fingerprint, and waits for your verification. After approval, that host and port are pinned to the accepted key.
